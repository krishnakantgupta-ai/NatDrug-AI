
## NatDrug-AI v2 — Research Validation

NatDrug-AI v2 extends the original platform with reproducible
machine-learning benchmarking, scaffold-aware cross-validation,
applicability-domain assessment, and natural-product screening.

### Research Highlights

- 3,619 curated EGFR bioactivity compounds
- Random Forest scaffold-CV R²: 0.6454
- ExtraTrees scaffold-CV R²: 0.6504
- 18,030 natural products screened from IMPPAT
- Model robustness and prediction-interval analysis

**Important:** Independent experimental natural-product validation
has not yet been established. Screening predictions are exploratory.
